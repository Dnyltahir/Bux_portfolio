The creative behind the mark. Muhammad Bux Mangi is an interdisciplinary artist and designer from Lahore who gets excited about big ideas and builds the visual systems that bring them to life.

Portrait placeholder

Bux studied BS Interdisciplinary Extended Design and Arts at Beaconhouse National University, Lahore — a programme that treats design as one connected practice rather than separate crafts.

That shows in the work: a logo is designed with its packaging in mind, and the packaging with the page it will be printed on. His practice covers identity design, brand design, packaging design and layout design.
